Notes separated by spaces, each NOTE:BEATS:SYLLABLE.

NOTEa pitch name: A to G, then # or b, then the octave (C4 is middle C); or R for a rest
BEATSlength in beats: 1, 0.5, 1.5, 2 ... the tempo slider sets the beats per minute
SYLLABLEthe sounds of one syllable, ARPAbet joined by dots: T.W.IH.NG. Its first vowel is held for the note; the sounds before it start ahead of the beat, those after it close the note.
restR:2 is two beats of silence
soundsthe same set as Speak: IY IH EH AE AA AO UH UW AH ER AX, EY OW AY AW OY, P B T D K G CH JH F V TH DH S Z SH ZH HH M N NG L R W Y
